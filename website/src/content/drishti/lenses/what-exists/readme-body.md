## How to use this lens

1. **Name the phenomenon** in one plain sentence.
2. **List parts** — physical, informational, social.
3. **Ask what would break** if people stopped believing it exists.
4. **Separate signal from container** — the message vs the medium.

## Mini-examples

- A **dollar bill** exists as paper, ink, and a shared ledger of trust.
- A **meeting** exists as calendar entries, people in a room, and an agenda in minds.
- A **database row** exists as magnetic domains and as a contract between services.

## Curriculum bridge
