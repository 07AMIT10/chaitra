## Uncertain tuples

<Callout variant="note" title="How to read this topic">
  Follow the narrative first, then open the **Lab** section for **uncertain tuples with confidence
  scores**. Browse **Sensors** or **Users** tables, pick an **EXISTS** or **SELECT** query, drag
  **confidence threshold τ**, read per-row **P(match query)**, try **Kitchen EXISTS**, **Threshold
  mistake**, or **Users age > 30** presets, predict whether **P(high temp in Kitchen) &gt; 95%**, then
  reveal **probabilistic OR ≈ 98%** vs **deterministic conf ≥ 50%**.
</Callout>
