## Windowed aggregates & watermarks

<Callout variant="note" title="How to read this topic">
  Follow the narrative first, then open the **Lab** section to scrub **ingest progress** on a synthetic
  mobile clickstream. Watch **tumbling event-time windows** accumulate totals, the **watermark** close each
  window when <code>max(event time) − Δ</code> passes its end, and late tunnel events get **dropped** after
  close. Try **Tunnel late (wide Δ)**, **Tight watermark**, or **Before window close** presets, then predict
  whether emitted window totals match hindsight ground truth before revealing the compare panel.
</Callout>
